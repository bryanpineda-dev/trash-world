Preserve its specific silhouette, refined pixel clusters, olive/yellow-green moss, warm stone highlights and cool blue-green shadows. Reconstruct the few occluded feet/base pixels coherently. Full object isolated and centered with transparent empty space around every edge, no scenery behind it, no ground strip, no other graves, no lanterns, no crows, no interface, no text. Orthographic side-view game prop, shallow frontal volume as in the reference, NOT isometric, NOT a 3D render or smooth painting. Crisp rich pixel art, tonal colored contours rather than black outline. Intended native size about 68 wide x 162 high pixels; details must remain readable at this game scale. Do not simplify it into a generic icon. Keep the statue solemn and delicate like the reference, not scary or grotesque. Actual transparent alpha background.

## Limites

La cruz generada conserva una inclinacion menor a la solicitada; se usa como cruz fracturada, no se afirma una copia geometrica exacta. La proporcion final se deriva del contenido real de cada original, no se deforma para coincidir con el tamano sugerido en el prompt. Las cinco piezas son estaticas: no incluyen cuervos animados, velas ni interacciones nuevas.
